Healthy Expense

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Your financial records stay under your control.

Healthy Expense stores your records on your device. We do not receive your financial entries through the app, run advertising, or track you across apps and websites. You choose whether to export or share a backup.

1. About this policy

This policy describes how Healthy Expense for iOS handles information when you use the app. “We” and “us” refer to the developer identified in the Contact section below. No registration or developer-hosted account is required.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves information you enter or import so it can organise your finances and display budgets, balances, and reports. This includes:

  • Income and expense titles, amounts, dates, categories, planned and actual entries, and transfers between your accounts.
  • Account names, bank or institution labels, account types, currencies, opening or carry-over balances, and tracking months.
  • Custom categories and currencies, appearance choices, and other app and report preferences.
  • A local indication of whether your Pro purchase is unlocked.

These records are processed locally. The app does not connect to your bank, request banking credentials, or upload your financial records to servers operated by us. It does not provide its own cloud synchronisation service.

3. Imports, exports, and device backups

Pro features allow you to export a JSON backup using the iOS share sheet and import a backup file you select. Exports contain financial records and included settings in readable, unencrypted form. Importing copies the selected file into app storage and replaces the app records covered by the backup.

If you save or share an export with another app, person, or cloud storage provider, that recipient receives the file at your direction. Their handling of the file is governed by their own privacy practices. We do not receive a copy unless you choose to send one to us.

Depending on your device settings, app data may also be included in iCloud or computer backups managed by Apple or by you. These backups are separate from any service operated by us.

4. Apple purchases and services

Apple processes Pro purchases through the App Store. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to verify purchase entitlement, unlock Pro, and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details through the app.

Apple may process purchase, usage, and diagnostic information under its own settings and policies, and may make sales reports or diagnostics available to developers. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details about Apple’s handling of information.

5. Advertising, analytics, and permissions

The app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

The app uses the system file picker and share sheet when you choose to import or export. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, or health data. The backup feature does not read your photo library; a sharing destination you select may request its own permissions.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your records remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app’s data. You can view and edit your records in the app and delete individual entries. To remove all local app data on iOS, use Delete App; offloading the app preserves its documents and data.

Deleting entries or the app does not delete files you exported, copies held by recipients, device backups, or Apple’s purchase records. Manage those copies through the relevant Files location, backup settings, recipient, or Apple service. Restoring a backup may restore previously saved records.

We cannot access, retrieve, or remotely delete records stored only on your device. You can stop using import and export at any time and manage system permissions in iOS Settings.

7. Security

The app relies on iOS app storage and device security protections. It does not add a separate encryption layer to its financial records or exported JSON files. Protect your device with a passcode and share backups only with people and services you trust.

8. Contact

If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us. Please avoid sending financial records unless needed for your request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its privacy practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for changes to how information is handled.
